Salmon Farming in Tasmania

The issue

Salmon farming is an important industry in Tasmania. It provides jobs and contributes to the Tasmanian economy. However, the industry is also facing controversy because of concerns about its impact on the marine environment.

In this project, you will research the debate surrounding salmon farming in Tasmania and investigate the following question — then develop your own informed conclusion.

"How should Tasmania balance the economic benefits of salmon farming with concerns about the environment?"

1 The salmon farming industry ›
  • Where is salmon farmed in Tasmania?
  • What type of salmon is farmed?
  • How large is the industry?
  • Why is salmon farming important to Tasmania?
  • What products does the industry produce?
2 Jobs and the economy ›
  • How many people work in the industry?
  • What types of jobs does it provide?
  • How does it support regional communities?
  • What other businesses benefit from the industry?
  • How does salmon farming contribute to the Tasmanian economy?
3 Environmental concerns ›
  • What happens to the marine environment around salmon farms?
  • What happens to waste and uneaten food?
  • What impact could farms have on water quality?
  • What are the concerns about fish health and disease?
  • What impact could salmon farming have on wild marine animals?
  • Are there concerns about antibiotics, chemicals or other treatments?
4 The salmon industry ›
  • What regulations control salmon farming?
  • What environmental monitoring takes place?
  • What changes has the industry made?
  • What technologies or practices are being introduced?
  • What does the industry say about its environmental performance?
